# Improvements

Planned features for [Track My Roadmap](https://www.trackmyroadmap.com/). The goal is to grow it from one personal Software Engineer roadmap into a platform where creators publish journeys and students follow them.

## Planned features

1. **Allow creators to create journeys.** A creator builds a journey from phases, topics and resources, then publishes it.
2. **Allow people to subscribe to a journey.** A student subscribes, and their progress is tracked and synced to their account.
3. **Allow creators to create journeys using AI and MCP.** A creator can draft a journey from a prompt, or create and update one from an AI assistant through an MCP server.
4. **Allow creators to monetize a journey.** A creator can make a journey free or paid.
5. **Allow creators to view full analytics of subscriber progress on a journey.** For example: subscribers, completion by phase, where people stop, and active learners.
6. **A dedicated page for students to explore all published free and paid journeys, like a marketplace.** Students can browse, search and filter journeys, then open one to see its details before subscribing.
